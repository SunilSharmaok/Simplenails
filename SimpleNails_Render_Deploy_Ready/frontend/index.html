Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our photo editing service

What photo formats do you support?

We support all major image formats including JPG, PNG, RAW files (from most cameras), and more. If you have a specific format, please contact us before submitting.

How secure is my photo data?

All uploads are encrypted and stored securely. We sign NDAs on request and delete all files after project completion to ensure your privacy.

What is your revision policy?

We offer up to two free revisions per project to ensure you're completely satisfied. Additional revisions may incur a small fee depending on the complexity.

Will my photos lose quality during editing?

No, we use professional editing software and techniques that preserve your image quality. For certain edits, we may even be able to enhance the overall quality.

Can I request specific editing styles?

Absolutely! You can provide reference images, detailed instructions, or even sample edits to help our editors understand exactly what you're looking for.

Do you offer bulk discounts?

Yes, we offer significant discounts for bulk orders. Contact us with your project details, and we'll provide a custom quote with bulk pricing.
